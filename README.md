# SpamBlock – Android Mobile Security App

**Block the unwanted. Protect what matters.**

SpamBlock is an Android mobile security application designed to help users protect themselves from unwanted phone calls, suspicious SMS messages, and potentially dangerous links.

Our goal is to make everyday mobile security simple, accessible, and easy to understand while giving users more control over their phone communications.

## 🛡️ What Is SpamBlock?

SpamBlock focuses on three important areas of everyday mobile security:

### 📞 1. Unwanted Call Protection

Helps users manage unwanted and potentially suspicious phone calls.

- Protection against unwanted calls
- Helps users identify suspicious phone numbers
- More control over incoming calls

### 💬 2. Suspicious SMS Protection

Helps users recognize text messages that may be suspicious or potentially harmful.

- Identification of suspicious SMS messages
- Increased awareness of potentially risky messages
- Support for safer messaging habits

### 🔗 3. Risky Link Protection

Helps users recognize potentially dangerous links encountered while using the internet.

- Protection against potentially risky links
- Helps users make more informed decisions before opening links
- Supports safer browsing habits

## 🎯 Our Mission

We believe users should have more control over who can reach them and what they interact with on their phones.

SpamBlock aims to provide a modern, straightforward, and trustworthy mobile security experience for everyday use.
